Pick your dates and find school-holiday camps around Lisbon, Cascais, Sintra, Oeiras and Almada, for kids aged 3 to 14.

Quick fill:

Confirmed and coming soon. A camp is listed as confirmed once it has published its exact dates. Camps that usually run during the upcoming break but haven't confirmed exact days yet appear under Coming soon, with last season's details clearly labelled. Camps for later breaks stay hidden until that break comes up. The list is checked monthly.

Prices are per child, as each camp publishes them. Totals for your dates are estimates from the published weekly or daily price. "Not published" means we couldn't find a price online. Camp details are refreshed monthly.
